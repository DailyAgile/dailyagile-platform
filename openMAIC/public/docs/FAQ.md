# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Quick answers to common questions about DailyAgile Platform.

## Account & Login

**Q: How do I create an account?**
A: Click "Sign Up" on the homepage, enter your name, email, and password (6+ characters). You'll receive a verification code by email (or see it in console for testing).

**Q: I forgot my password, what do I do?**
A: Password reset is not yet available. Please contact support at support@dailyagile.com and we'll help you reset your account.

**Q: Can I use the same email for multiple accounts?**
A: No, each email can only be registered once. If you need a second account, use a different email address.

**Q: How long does my login session last?**
A: Your session lasts 24 hours. After that, you'll need to log in again. This is for security.

**Q: Is my password encrypted?**
A: Yes, passwords are hashed with SHA256 encryption before being stored. They cannot be recovered or viewed, even by administrators.

## Quizzes & Assignments

**Q: What's a quiz code?**
A: A 4-5 character code (like `QA1B2`) that your instructor uses to share a quiz with you. Enter it on the DailyAgile homepage to access the quiz.

**Q: Can I take a quiz multiple times?**
A: Yes, most instructors allow unlimited retakes. Check your course syllabus to confirm. Your highest score is usually recorded.

**Q: What if I accidentally submit before finishing?**
A: Once submitted, you cannot change your answers. However, you can usually retake the quiz unless your instructor has disabled that feature.

**Q: Can I pause a quiz and come back later?**
A: Currently, you must complete a quiz in one sitting. It usually takes 15-30 minutes depending on the number of questions. Plan your time accordingly.

**Q: What if my internet drops during a quiz?**
A: Your progress is auto-saved. Log back in and you can continue from where you left off (exact progress depends on when the connection dropped).

**Q: How do I know if I passed?**
A: After submitting, you'll see your score (e.g., 18/20) and percentage. Your instructor sets the passing percentage, usually 70-80%. You'll see a "PASSED" or "FAILED" indicator.

**Q: Can I see the correct answers after submitting?**
A: Yes! The results page shows your answer, the correct answer, and the instructor's explanation for each question.

**Q: Can I retake a quiz immediately?**
A: Usually yes, but your instructor might have cooldown periods (e.g., must wait 24 hours between attempts). Check your course details.

**Q: What if my instructor says I should have passed but the quiz says I failed?**
A: Contact your instructor directly. They can manually override scores or review your answers with you.

## Scores & Results

**Q: When will I see my results?**
A: Immediately after submitting. Automatic quizzes are scored instantly. Essay questions (if used) may take longer if manually graded by your instructor.

**Q: Can I see my progress in the course?**
A: Yes, check your student dashboard. It shows completed quizzes, scores, and progress toward course completion.

**Q: How are my scores calculated?**
A: Each question has a point value. Your total score is points earned ÷ total possible points. Some instructors weight questions differently.

**Q: Can other students see my score?**
A: No, your scores are private. Only you and your instructor can see them.

**Q: Will my score affect my GPA?**
A: That depends on your school's policies. Ask your instructor how these quizzes affect your grade.

**Q: Can I get a transcript of my scores?**
A: Yes, you can usually download your results as a PDF or CSV. Ask your instructor for this option.

## Deadlines & Extensions

**Q: How do I know when a quiz is due?**
A: Your instructor will set a due date. It appears on your dashboard next to the quiz. Calendar notifications may also be sent (if your instructor enabled that).

**Q: What if I miss a deadline?**
A: Contact your instructor immediately. Many instructors allow late submissions with possible point deductions. Extensions are case-by-case.

**Q: How do I request a deadline extension?**
A: Go to your "My Assignments" page, find the assignment, and click "Request Extension." Your instructor will review and approve or deny the request.

**Q: How long can I extend my deadline?**
A: That's up to your instructor. Request a reasonable timeframe (e.g., 3-5 days extra). Be honest about why you need more time.

**Q: Can I request an extension after the deadline has passed?**
A: It's better to request before the deadline. After-the-fact requests may not be honored. Contact your instructor directly if this happens.

**Q: Will an extension affect my grade?**
A: Usually no - extensions just give you more time. Your instructor may apply late penalties depending on their policy.

## Technical Issues

**Q: The quiz page won't load. What do I do?**
A: 
1. Try refreshing the page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
2. Clear your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
3. Try a different browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox)
4. Check your internet connection
5. If still stuck, contact support

**Q: I got an error "Quiz Not Found." What does that mean?**
A: The quiz code doesn't exist or the quiz was deleted. Double-check your code with your instructor and try again.

**Q: The quiz timer is running fast/slow. What's wrong?**
A: Timers are server-side, so your local time shouldn't matter. However, if you see unusual behavior, try refreshing the page. If the timer is wildly off, contact support.

**Q: My answers weren't saved. What happened?**
A: This is rare but can happen if:
- Your internet dropped
- You closed the browser before submitting
- The server had an issue
Contact support with details (what questions, when it happened). We can usually recover your progress.

**Q: Can I take the quiz on my phone?**
A: Yes, the platform is mobile-friendly. Use a mobile browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android).

**Q: Do I need to download anything to take a quiz?**
A: No, everything works in your web browser. No plugins or downloads required.

## Certificates & Completion

**Q: Will I get a certificate when I finish?**
A: Yes, if you complete all required quizzes with passing scores. The certificate is automatically generated and available for download.

**Q: What format is the certificate in?**
A: Certificates are PDF files that you can download, print, or share digitally.

**Q: Can I share my certificate on LinkedIn?**
A: Yes! Most certificates include LinkedIn badge integration. Follow the instructions on your certificate to add it to your profile.

**Q: Is the certificate recognized by employers?**
A: That depends on the course and your employer. DailyAgile certificates show completion of our training program. Talk to your employer about whether they accept these credentials.

**Q: Can I get a printed certificate?**
A: You can download the PDF and print it yourself. Some courses may offer printed certificates through your institution.

**Q: How long is the certificate valid?**
A: Most certificates don't expire. They represent completion at a point in time. Your individual scores may be reviewed years later if needed.

## Instructor Features

**Q: Can I see all my students' scores at once?**
A: Yes. As an instructor, go to "View Results" on any quiz to see a summary of all students' scores.

**Q: Can I export student scores to Excel?**
A: Yes, the results page has a "Download CSV" option. You can open that in Excel.

**Q: Can I manually adjust a student's score?**
A: Yes, you can add instructor feedback and override scores if needed. This is useful for essay questions or special circumstances.

**Q: How do I delete a quiz?**
A: Go to your quiz list and click "Delete." This is a soft delete - the quiz is hidden but can be restored if needed.

**Q: Can I duplicate a quiz to create a new one?**
A: Yes, click "Duplicate" on any quiz in your list. You'll get an exact copy with a new code.

**Q: Can I edit a quiz after students start taking it?**
A: No, editing is locked once submissions arrive. Create a new version if you need to make changes.

## Privacy & Security

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes. We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS) and secure authentication. Your passwords are hashed and cannot be recovered.

**Q: What data do you collect?**
A: We collect:
- Your name and email (for account)
- Quiz responses and scores (for grading)
- Login history (for security)
We do NOT collect device info, location, or browsing behavior.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**
A: Yes, email support@dailyagile.com to request account deletion. Your data will be permanently removed.

**Q: Who can see my quiz responses?**
A: Only you and your instructor. Other students cannot see your responses or scores.

**Q: Is my data backed up?**
A: Yes, all data is continuously backed up. In case of data loss, we can restore from backups.

**Q: Do you use my data for advertising?**
A: No, we don't sell or share your data. Your data is only used for the platform's functionality.

**Q: Is there a privacy policy?**
A: Yes, see our Privacy Policy (linked in the footer of the website).
