## 📞 Contact & Support

Need help? We're here for you!

### Support Options

1. **Email:** support@dailyagile.com
   - Response time: 24 hours (business days)
   - Best for complex issues
   - Include: your email, course name, what happened

2. **FAQ & Help Center:**
   - Instant answers to common questions
   - Available 24/7
   - Search using Ctrl+F

3. **Documentation:**
   - Comprehensive guides for all features
   - Step-by-step tutorials
   - Troubleshooting guides

### What to Include in Your Email

Help us help you faster:

- Your email address
- Course/quiz name (if applicable)
- What you were doing when the issue occurred
- Error message (if any)
- Screenshots (if applicable)
- Your browser and device

Example:
```
Subject: Can't submit quiz

Hi,

I'm trying to submit Quiz QA1B2 but getting an error.

Details:
- Email: student@example.com
- Quiz code: QA1B2
- Error message: "500 Internal Server Error"
- Browser: Chrome on Windows
- When: 2026-08-12 at 2:30 PM

I answered all 20 questions but clicking Submit doesn't work.

Thanks!
```
