SENSITIVE TABLES TRACKED
================================================================================

1. webhook_audit_logs
   - What: All INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE operations
   - Why: Detect tampering of PCI compliance audit trail
   - Auto-logged: ✅ Yes (via trigger)

2. feature_flags
   - What: All flag changes (enable/disable, rollout %)
   - Why: Detect unauthorized kill switches
   - Auto-logged: ✅ Yes (via trigger)

3. email_queue
   - What: Status changes and deletions
   - Why: Monitor email operations and suspicious changes
   - Auto-logged: ✅ Yes (status/retry_count only, not heartbeats)

  - idx_admin_audit_action (action, created_at)
  - idx_admin_audit_record (table_name, row_id, created_at)
