## Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Go to Sign Up

1. Open DailyAgile in your browser
2. Click **Sign Up** button

### Step 2: Enter Your Details

Fill in the form with:
- **First Name**: Your first name
- **Last Name**: Your last name
- **Email**: Your email address (must be unique)
- **Password**: At least 6 characters

Example:
```
First Name: John
Last Name: Doe
Email: john.doe@example.com
Password: MyPassword123
```

### Step 3: Verify Your Email

After signing up:
1. Check your email for the verification code
2. In development/testing: Check your console (code is printed there)
3. Copy the 6-character code
4. Paste it into the verification form
5. Click **Verify**

**Note:** Verification codes expire after 10 minutes. If yours expired, click **Resend Code**.

## Logging In

### First Time Login

1. Click **Login** on the homepage
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **Sign In**
4. You'll be taken to your student dashboard

### Staying Logged In

Your session stays active for 24 hours. You'll automatically be logged out after that for security.

**Forgot your password?** Currently, reset is not available. [Contact support](mailto:support@dailyagile.com) if you need help.

## Finding a Quiz

### Option 1: Using a Quiz Code (Recommended)

Your instructor will give you a **Quiz Code** (looks like: `QA1B2`).

1. From your dashboard, click **Enter Quiz Code**
2. Type the code (case doesn't matter)
3. Click **Continue**
4. The quiz details will load

### Option 2: From Your Dashboard

If you're enrolled in a classroom:
1. Go to **My Classrooms**
2. Select your classroom
3. You'll see all available quizzes
4. Click a quiz to start

### Option 3: Direct Link

If your instructor shared a link like:
```
https://dailyagile.com/student/quiz/QA1B2
```

Just click it - you'll be taken directly to the quiz.

## Taking a Quiz

### Before You Start

- Make sure you have **15-30 minutes** of uninterrupted time
- Close other applications and notifications
- Have paper nearby if you need to work through math problems
- Your instructor may have a time limit - check the quiz details

### During the Quiz

1. **Read the question** carefully
2. **Select your answer** from options A, B, C, D, or E
3. **Click Next** to move to the next question
4. You can go back to change answers (some quizzes allow this)
5. The timer shows how much time you have left (if applicable)

### Features While Taking a Quiz

| Feature | What It Does |
|---------|------------|
| Progress Bar | Shows how many questions you've completed |
| Timer | Displays remaining time (if quiz has a time limit) |
| Back Button | Go to previous question and change answer |
| Question Number | Shows which question you're on (e.g., "Question 3 of 20") |

### Tips for Success

- **Read all options** before selecting
- **Don't second-guess** yourself too much
- **Use the timer** to pace yourself (5 min per 5 questions is typical)
- **Review at the end** if time permits

## Submitting Your Quiz

### When Ready to Submit

1. Answer all remaining questions
2. You'll see a **Review** screen showing all your answers
3. Make final changes if needed
4. Click **Submit Quiz**
5. Confirm by clicking **Submit** again

**Important:** Once submitted, you cannot change your answers. Make sure you're ready!

---

## Viewing Your Results

After submitting:

1. **Instant Results Page** will show:
   - Your score: `X out of Y points`
   - Percentage: `Z%`
   - Whether you passed

2. **Your Answers Review:**
   - Each question and your answer
   - Correct answer highlighted
   - Explanation from instructor

3. **Next Steps:**
   - If you passed: ✅ Certificate available (if full course complete)
   - If you didn't pass: Option to retake (if allowed by instructor)

### Understanding Your Score

```
Score: 18 out of 20 points = 90% ✅ PASSED
Score: 12 out of 20 points = 60% ❌ FAILED (varies by class)
```

Your instructor sets the passing percentage. Check your syllabus if unsure.

## Requesting an Extension

If you need more time to complete an assignment:

### How to Request

1. From your **My Assignments** page
2. Find the assignment with a due date
3. Click **Request Extension**
4. Fill in:
   - **New Due Date**: When you can complete it
   - **Reason** (optional): Why you need more time
5. Click **Submit Request**

### What Happens Next

- Your instructor gets a notification
- They can approve or deny your request
- You'll receive an email with their decision
- If approved, new deadline is updated

### Important Notes

- Request **before** the due date, not after
- Extensions are at instructor's discretion
- Provide a reasonable new date (e.g., 3-5 days later)
- Be honest about your situation

## Troubleshooting

### "Quiz Not Found" Error

- Check your quiz code is exactly correct
- Ask your instructor to resend the code
- Verify the quiz hasn't been deleted

### "Can't Submit Quiz" Error

- Make sure you've answered all questions
- Try refreshing the page
- Check your internet connection
- [Contact support](mailto:support@dailyagile.com)

### Lost My Verification Code

- Click **Resend Code** on the verification page
- New code will be sent to your email
- Codes expire after 10 minutes
- Try again with the new code

### Stuck on a Question

- You can skip and come back (if allowed)
- Try the answer you think is most likely
- Don't leave it blank (you won't get points anyway)
- Move on - you can review at the end

### Quiz Timed Out

- Save your progress frequently
- Don't have too many tabs open
- Close other applications
- Use a modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I take a quiz multiple times?**
A: Depends on your instructor's settings. Check the quiz details.

**Q: Can I pause a quiz?**
A: Not currently - you must complete it in one sitting. Plan your time accordingly.

**Q: What if I submit by accident?**
A: Once submitted, you cannot change answers. Contact your instructor if this happens.

**Q: How long are my results available?**
A: Your results are saved permanently in your dashboard.

**Q: Can I see other students' scores?**
A: No - your scores are private. Only your instructor can see them.

**Q: What if my internet drops during a quiz?**
A: Your progress is auto-saved. Log back in and continue from where you left off.

**Q: How do I get a certificate?**
A: Complete all quizzes in your course with passing scores. Certificate is generated automatically.
